Good to know

Questions patients often ask

Cannot find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp or ask the demo assistant at the bottom right.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so you are seen without waiting. For dental pain or an emergency, call us and we will try to fit you in the same day.

Is dental treatment painful?

We use modern anaesthesia and gentle technique so that most treatments are comfortable. Tell us if you feel anxious and we will go at your pace.

How often should I get a check-up?

Most people benefit from a check-up and cleaning every six months. Your dentist may suggest a different interval based on your needs.

Can children be treated here?

Yes. We welcome children of all ages and we recommend a first visit around the time the first teeth appear.
